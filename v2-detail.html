How to auto-rig a character in Meshy

Take a static character mesh and turn it into a fully rigged model ready for Mixamo or Blender — without leaving Meshy. 6 steps, ~10 minutes.

What you'll need

  • A character mesh (from Image to 3D or Text to 3D)
  • A Meshy account on the Free plan or above
  • Optionally: Blender 3.6+ or Mixamo for downstream animation

Step 1 — Open your model

From your Workspace library, click the character you want to rig. In the right panel, choose Rigging from the toolbar.

Step 2 — Choose rig type

Pick the skeleton template that matches your character:

  • Humanoid — bipedal characters with arms and legs.
  • Quadruped — animals, creatures.
  • Custom — upload your own skeleton.

Tip

If your character is stylized (long limbs, large head), Humanoid still works — joints will be auto-fit to your mesh proportions.

Step 3 — Confirm joint placement

Meshy predicts joint positions. Inspect them in the 3D viewer; drag any joint to refine. The mirror toggle keeps left/right symmetric.

Step 4 — Generate the rig

Click Generate rig. Rigging usually completes in 20–40 seconds. You'll get a fully skinned mesh with bone hierarchy.

Step 5 — Test with a pose preset

Apply one of the built-in pose presets (T-pose, A-pose, walk) to verify the rig deforms cleanly. If something looks off:

  • Reopen Step 3 and adjust the joint position
  • Or enable Refine skin weights in the rig panel

Step 6 — Export

Click Export and choose:

  • FBX with skin weights — for Blender / Maya / Unity / Unreal.
  • Send to Mixamo — opens the model directly in Mixamo.
  • GLB — for web (Three.js, real-time previews).

FAQ

Does this work for non-humanoid characters?

Yes — pick the Quadruped rig type for animals, or use the Custom skeleton option to upload your own bone hierarchy.

Can I edit the rig manually?

After export to FBX you can edit in Blender or Maya; the skin weights are preserved.

Why are my fingers not deforming?

The base auto-rig includes only the main hand joint. For finger control, enable the "Finger rig" add-on before generating.
